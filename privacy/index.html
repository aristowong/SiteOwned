What we collect

Contact details you send (name, business name, email, optional phone). Update emails and attachments you send about the public website. Course progress, quiz scores, and sign-in tokens if you take the course. Payment card data is handled by Stripe, not stored on our site. We may keep minimal request logs (page, time, success or failure) without a marketing profile.

How we use it

To reply, build or edit the site you asked for, run the course, and keep records of approval. Email text may be sent to an AI provider to draft an edit. A person can be copied when a request is risky. We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Retention

Intake and approval mail is kept while you are a client and then for up to 24 months, unless a longer period is required for tax. Course progress is kept while your login exists. You can email us to delete a course record.

Your choices

Email hello@siteowned.ai to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold. We will not discriminate against you for asking. An authorized agent may write for you if they show permission.

Subprocessors

GitHub (site files), Cloudflare (hosting, email routing, the worker), Stripe (payments), the AI provider named in your plan (drafting edits and course help), and the email provider that delivers mail (Cloudflare Email Routing, and Resend only if we turn it on).

AI

Most drafting is AI. The model sees the edit request and the page text needed to make the patch. It is not used to make decisions about credit, housing, or health care.

Patient health information

SiteOwned does not collect or store patient health information. Clinic clients must not send PHI in email, attachments, forms, or the website. Intake of health information belongs on the practice's own tools. If we spot something that looks like PHI, we refuse the message and do not keep the body.
