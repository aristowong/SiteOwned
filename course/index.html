Questions

Questions about the DIY website course

What is the Own Your Site DIY course?

It is a DIY website course that teaches you to plan a site, generate it with AI, deploy it free, connect a domain, and cancel a monthly builder plan. The course is $49. Templates are $89. A 45-minute done-with-you call is $199.

How is AI used in the course?

You use AI to generate the pages from your own facts, then you read every line before it is published. Later edits are also described to AI. The files stay yours.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The course follows plain steps: write, generate, deploy, connect the domain. You should be comfortable with email and a login.

Is there a track for a PT or OT private practice?

Yes. The bonus module is a ClinicSite track for physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech, and chiropractic practices. It is included in every tier. No patient data is stored on the site you build.
