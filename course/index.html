Questions

What the course is, and what it is not.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The course follows plain steps: write, generate, deploy, connect the domain. You should be comfortable with email and a login.

Which tier should I pick?

The $49 course is the lessons. $89 adds starter templates for a small business and a clinic. $199 adds a 45-minute call to work on your site together. The call is not a done-for-you build, and it does not include the templates unless we agree that in the email.

Is there material for clinicians?

Yes. The bonus module is a ClinicSite track for PT, OT, speech, and chiropractic practices. It is included in every tier.

How do I pay?

Email us. This page has no checkout. We reply with payment instructions and access. Nothing on the form is stored on a server.

Can I still hire you afterward?

Yes. The small-business and ClinicSite packages are there if you would rather have the site done for you.
