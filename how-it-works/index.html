Updates

Email the change. Reply YES to publish.

Most of the edit is AI. You still approve it. Email the address on your plan. We reply with a short summary and a preview link. Reply YES and the live site updates. Reply with what to change and you get a new preview. Reply UNDO to put back the last published version.

The four steps

  1. 01

    Email

    Write the edit in a normal email. A new paragraph, hours, a photo, a link, or a name.

  2. 02

    Preview

    You get a link. Look at it on your phone. Nothing is public until you say so.

  3. 03

    YES

    Reply YES to publish. Any other reply revises the same draft.

  4. 04

    Live

    The reply is "Your site is updated." UNDO reverts that publish.

Texting is coming soon. Email is the way to send an update today. Clinic clients: do not email patient details. No patient data is stored on the site.
