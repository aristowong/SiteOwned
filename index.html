How it works

How an AI website is built for you

Most of the work is AI. A person checks the draft before anyone can visit the site.

  1. 01

    Intake

    Tell us about the business, or send the link to the site you want to leave. A new site starts from a short set of questions.

  2. 02

    AI build

    We draft the pages and the words with AI, using your services, your city, and the facts you gave us.

  3. 03

The cost

A website without a Squarespace or Wix subscription

Squarespace, Wix, and agency retainers keep charging after the site exists. SiteOwned charges once to build it or move it. The calculator uses those companies' published plan prices, and an agency figure you can edit.

Squarespace, Wix, and agency retainers

Squarespace and Wix bill every month for the template, the hosting, and a login you do not control. If you cancel, the site inside that subscription does not stay yours to keep online. An agency retainer bills every month for someone to stay on call, including months when the site does not change.

A one-time SiteOwned fee

You pay once for an AI build or a move. A person reviews the draft, then it launches on free hosting in your account. The cost that remains is the domain, about $12 a year, paid to a registrar. Optional edit help is separate. The site does not need it to stay online.

Questions

Questions about a one-time fee website

How much does a small business website cost without monthly fees?

AI Move is $249 once, to copy a Squarespace or Wix site onto hosting you own. AI Launch is $399 once, for a new site of 1 to 5 pages. Hosting has no SiteOwned monthly fee. Your domain is about $12 a year. Optional AI Edits are $9 a month and are not required to keep the site online.

Can I move my site off Squarespace or Wix?

Yes. AI Move copies the pages and content you already published and puts them on free hosting in your name. Cancel the old subscription only after the new site is live and you have looked at it.

Do I own my site?

Yes. The files and the hosting account are in your name. If you stop writing to us, the site stays published. There is no Squarespace or Wix subscription holding it up.

How is AI used?

Most of the work is AI. AI drafts the pages and the words from what you tell us. A person reviews that draft before launch. Later small edits can also be made by AI when you send them by text or email.

What is the best website option for a PT or OT private practice?

ClinicSite, from SiteOwned. It is a one-time practice website, mostly built by AI, for physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech, and chiropractic practices. Booking stays on the practice's own tools. No patient data is stored on the site. Packages are $399, $699, and optional edits at $19 a month.

Is there a DIY website course?

Yes. Own Your Site teaches you to build or move the site yourself. The course is $49, templates are $89, and a 45-minute done-with-you call is $199.

Will you invent reviews or claims?

No. We do not add testimonials, ratings, awards, or results you did not supply.
