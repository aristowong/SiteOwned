The cost

A monthly bill, or a site you own.

Builder subscriptions and agency retainers keep charging after the site exists. SiteOwned charges once to create it or move it.

Builder subscriptions and retainers

A website builder bills every month for the template, the hosting, and a login you do not control. Cancel the subscription, and the site that lived inside it typically comes down with the bill. An agency retainer bills for ongoing access to a team, including months when nothing on the site changes.

A one-time SiteOwned fee

You pay once for an AI build or a move. A person reviews the draft, then it launches on free hosting in your account. The cost that remains is the domain, about $12 a year, paid to a registrar. Optional edit help is separate. The site does not need it to stay online.

Questions

Plain answers before you write to us.

Who owns the website?

You do. The files and the hosting account are in your name. If you stop writing to us, the site stays published. There is no builder subscription holding it up.

Is the hosting free?

The hosting plan we use has no monthly fee for a brochure site of this size. You do not pay SiteOwned for hosting. Your domain is separate, about $12 a year, paid to the registrar.

How soon can it go live?

About 48 hours after we have your intake, or access to the site you want moved, and a decision on the domain. If a name, a logo, or a login is still missing, we wait for it.

What does the AI do, and what does a person do?

AI drafts the structure and the copy from what you tell us. A person reviews that draft before launch, so we are not publishing unchecked text.

Can I edit it later?

Yes. Change it yourself, or add AI Edits and send small updates by text or email. Edits are optional. Canceling them does not take the site down.

What if I want to do this myself?

The Own Your Site course teaches the same path: plan the site, generate it with AI, deploy it free, connect the domain, and keep editing.

Will you invent reviews or claims?

No. We do not add testimonials, ratings, awards, or results you did not supply.
