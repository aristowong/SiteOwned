Why practices use it

The marketing site stays a marketing site.

  1. No patient data lives on the site

    Intake goes through the practice’s own HIPAA-compliant tools. The website links out. Do not put clinical details on the marketing pages, and we will not ask you to.

  2. Booking stays where it is

    Jane, WebPT, SimplePractice, or another system you already trust keeps the appointments. The site’s button opens that link.

  3. Built by someone who has done clinical rollouts

    The founder has a background in deploying clinical technology into health systems and onboarding Fortune 500 clients. ClinicSite is run with that habit: a careful handoff, and no patient data sitting on a brochure site.

  4. The practice owns the website

    Same model as the rest of SiteOwned. One-time build, free hosting, domain in the practice’s name. Stopping optional edits does not unpublish the site.

Questions

Before a practice switches the domain.

Does any patient information live on the site?

No. The site holds public marketing pages. Intake goes through the practice’s own HIPAA-compliant tools. Do not send clinical details to the website.

Can you link Jane, WebPT, or SimplePractice?

Yes. AI Move and AI Launch connect the booking or EMR link you already give patients. The scheduler itself stays on that system.

What does an eight-page clinic site include?

Up to eight pages, drawn from services and conditions, provider bios, insurance, and visit information, with local wording such as “pediatric OT in Redondo Beach.”

Is the sample clinic a client?

No. Merrin & Quill Pediatric Therapy is fictional. The sample shows layout only. There are no client logos and no reviews on this site.

What if we already have a site we like?

AI Move puts that site on hosting the practice owns and reconnects the booking link and Google profile. It is a move, not a forced redesign.
