Why practices use it

No patient data stored on the site

  1. No patient data lives on the site

    No patient data is stored on the site. Intake goes through the practice's own HIPAA-compliant tools. The website links out. Do not put clinical details on the marketing pages, and we will not ask you to.

  2. Booking stays where it is

    Jane, WebPT, SimplePractice, or another system you already trust keeps the appointments. The site’s button opens that link.

  3. Built by someone who has done clinical rollouts

    The founder has a background in deploying clinical technology into health systems and onboarding Fortune 500 clients. ClinicSite is run with that habit: a careful handoff, and no patient data sitting on a brochure site.

  4. The practice owns the website

    Same model as the rest of SiteOwned. One-time build, free hosting, domain in the practice’s name. Stopping optional edits does not unpublish the site.
