Eight pages, when you need them

An AI Launch includes up to eight pages. This example keeps them on one screen so the shape is easy to scan. A launched site can split them apart.

  1. Home
  2. Occupational therapy
  3. Physical therapy
  4. Speech therapy
  5. Our therapists
  6. Insurance
  7. New patients
  8. Visit

New patients, on a real site, explains how to schedule and points at the practice’s own forms. It does not ask for health information on the website.
