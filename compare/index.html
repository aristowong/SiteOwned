What a subscription costs over time

Most of a SiteOwned site is AI. You pay once and you own the files. This page puts that next to Squarespace, Wix, or an agency estimate you can edit. It is arithmetic, not a promise about your old bill.

Prices as of 26 November 2026. Squarespace USD rates: pricing file (data dated 5 October 2026) and pricing page. Wix yearly prices: plans, website builder, and Wix Harmony cost note (US yearly rates as of 5 November 2026). The agency row is a typical estimate, not a published rate.

Questions

How is the savings number calculated?

The calculator adds the current option's bills for the years you pick, then subtracts the SiteOwned package and, if you turn it on, the care plan. A domain fee of about $12 a year is left out of both sides.

Are the Squarespace and Wix prices exact?

They are the USD figures published on Squarespace's pricing file and Wix's plan pages as of 26 November 2026. Wix's pages listed yearly prices, not a separate month-to-month amount. Plans change, so check the source links.

Is the agency number a real quote?

No. The setup field is a typical estimate, starting at the low end of $1,249 to $4,000. The monthly retainer starts at $0 until you type a number you were actually quoted.
