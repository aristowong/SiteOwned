# Bonus: ClinicSite track

This bonus is for physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech, and chiropractic sites. The public site lists services, bios, insurance notes, and how to book.

No patient data is stored on the site. Booking stays on the practice's own tools, such as Jane, WebPT, or SimplePractice. Do not put clinical details in the page or in an edit email.
