# Welcome & Setup

Most of this course is AI-driven. You still decide what is true.

You will need an email address, a folder for your pages, and the login for the domain you already have or plan to buy. The domain is about $12 a year, paid to a registrar. Hosting for a static site can be free.

Write down the business name, the city, and the one sentence that says what you do. Do not add claims, reviews, or numbers you cannot point to.
