# Export from Squarespace/Wix

If you already pay Squarespace or Wix, copy the words and the page list out before you cancel. Export or save the text, the photo files, and the menu of pages.

Cancel the old subscription only after the new site is live and you have looked at it. Canceling first can take the old site offline.
