AI Launch

Ten questions. Then we draft.

You do not need a brand book. These are the questions an AI Launch starts from. Photos help, and they are optional.

  1. Business name
  2. What you do, in one sentence
  3. Who it is for
  4. City or towns you serve
  5. Services to list
  6. Hours and how to get in touch
  7. Where people should book, call, or send a message
  8. Photos you already have, if any
  9. How the writing should sound
  10. Anything the site must not claim

Questions

Before you leave the old plan.

Is AI Move a redesign?

It is a move. We carry over the pages and content you already published on Wix or Squarespace and put them on hosting you own. If a widget only works inside the old builder, we will tell you before you cancel that plan.

What do I need for AI Launch?

Answers to the ten questions. They cover the business, who it is for, the services, how to get in touch, and anything the site must not say. Photos help, and a site can launch without them.

Do I have to pay $9 a month?

No. AI Edits is optional. The one-time package includes the build or the move, the review, and the launch.

When should I cancel the old subscription?

After the domain points to the new site and you have looked at it. Canceling first can take the old site offline before the new one is ready.

What if I have no photos?

We launch with type, color, and layout. You can add photos later, yourself or with an edit.
