AI Launch

Ten questions. Then we draft.

You do not need a brand book. These are the questions an AI Launch starts from. Photos help, and they are optional.

  1. Business name
  2. What you do, in one sentence
  3. Who it is for
  4. City or towns you serve
  5. Services to list
  6. Hours and how to get in touch

Questions

Moving a site off Squarespace or Wix

How much does a small business website cost without monthly fees?

AI Move is $249 once. AI Launch is $399 once. Hosting is free. The domain is about $12 a year, paid to a registrar. AI Edits are $9 a month, optional.

Can I move my site off Squarespace?

Yes. AI Move carries the pages and content from Squarespace onto hosting you own. It is a move, not a redesign. If a widget only works inside Squarespace, we say so before you cancel.

Can I move my site off Wix?

Yes. The same AI Move package, $249 once, copies a Wix site onto free hosting in your name.

Do I own my site?

Yes. The files sit in an account you control. Canceling optional edits does not take the site down.

How is AI used?

Most of the work is AI. AI writes the first draft from your ten answers, or from the site you are moving. A person checks names, hours, and anything the site must not say before it goes live.

Do I have to pay $9 a month?

No. AI Edits is optional. The one-time package includes the build or the move, the review, and the launch.

When should I cancel Squarespace or Wix?

After the domain points to the new site and you have looked at it. Canceling first can take the old site offline before the new one is ready.
